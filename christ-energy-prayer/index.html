The Christ Energy Prayer

By Carol Channell-Cox

Dear Mother/Father God I ask to be cleared and cleansed in the Gold Radiant Light In the Crystal Red Energy of the Earth Mother And in the Rose Magnificat

I seek communion and the grounding of that communion, And the alignment of all of the selves throughout the universes. I open myself to those beings of Light who would work with me For the co-creation of Heaven on Earth.

In the name of Jesus the Christ, and my own Christed consciousness, I command any and all negativity to be removed from here, To be encapsulated in its own light, Surrounded by the ultraviolet light, And returned to its source of emanation, Never again to re-establish here, in me, or in anyone.

I ask to be filled with the White Light of the Christ The green healing light of compassion And the purple transmuting light. I ask that this room be filled and surrounded with light And the six-pointed star of Golden Light. For this and all of my many blessings, I give thanks.

I seek to be used as a channel of light, love, and information Within Divine Will and for the highest good only. I accept those beings of Light who are guides and teachers. Those who would work with me and through me And I accept only those beings who walk and work within the Light.

Amen 🙏🏽
