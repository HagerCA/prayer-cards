A prayer he prays

Prayer of Acceptance

Traditional · author unknown

Translated from the Spanish Yo acepto mi Ser Perfecto.

I accept that the joy I have longed for is already in my life.

I accept that the love for which I have prayed is already within me.

I accept that the peace I have asked for is already my reality.

I accept that the abundance I have sought already fills my life.

In my truth, I accept my perfect Being. My lessons have been chosen carefully by my own Self.

And now I walk through them in full experience. My path leads me on a sacred journey with a divine purpose.

My experiences become part of All That Is.

To my knowledge, I accept my perfect Being. I accept that everything in my past was given in love.

I accept that everything at this moment comes from love. I accept that everything in my future will result from a greater love. In my Being, I accept my perfection.
