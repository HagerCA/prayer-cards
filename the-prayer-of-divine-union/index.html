A prayer for two

The Prayer of Divine Union

Christopher Hager

Divine Union, Source of All Love and Light,

In this sacred moment, we come before you with hearts wide open, seeking your guidance and blessings. We acknowledge the profound gift of love that has brought our souls together on this sacred path. We recognize the beauty and significance of our journey thus far, and we humbly ask for your divine intervention.

May this heart-opening medicine, bestowed upon us beloved souls, be a catalyst for deeper connection, truth, love, and gratitude. May it dissolve any barriers or limitations that have held us back from fully embracing our authentic selves and each other.

As we ingest this medicine, may it infuse our beings with a profound sense of unity, compassion, and understanding. May it open our hearts to receive with love the truth of each other’s feelings and perspectives. May it bless our voice with the ability to share our truths from a place of love. May it allow us to explore the vastness of our souls, intertwining in a dance of pure loving devotion.

May this medicine be a gentle guide, illuminating the path of our journey together. May it help us navigate through the twists and turns, offering clarity and insight when faced with challenges or uncertainties. May it remind us of the sacred bond we share, as we traverse the sacred realms of love and union.

We offer our deepest gratitude for this transformative medicine and for the opportunity to witness our sacred union from a higher perspective. We trust in your divine wisdom, dear Universe (God), to bless our souls with an abundance of love, joy, and harmony. May our hearts be forever intertwined in a tapestry of divine love, for the highest good of all.

With boundless love and gratitude, we surrender this prayer to your divine hands.

Amen.
Aho
Ase
