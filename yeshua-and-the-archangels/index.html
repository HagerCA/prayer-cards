Invocations · Christopher Hager

Yeshua and the Archangels

The centreYeshua, the Christ Anointed

Yeshua Ben Joseph, the Christ Anointed, I call upon you to fill my heart full of love, kindness, compassion, and compersion. Allow me to see the face of God in everyone I meet. To fill the hearts of those around me as you have filled mine. Bringing joy, light, and love everywhere I go.

In green, with the staffArchangel Raphael

“Archangel Raphael, healer of God, guide of travelers, companion through crossings. Walk with me today as I walk with others. Let what moves through me bring healing rather than performance. Let me see clearly what wants to be seen and say only what wants to be said.”

In gold, sword raisedArchangel Michael

Archangel Michael, sword raised. Cut through the fog. Cut through the lies. Let only truth remain. Let it move through my heart and out into the world. I am ready to see. I am ready to speak. Stand with me.
